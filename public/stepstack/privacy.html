Stepstack Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

In short

  • Stepstack records a website flow only when you start a recording, and only in that tab and the tabs or pop-ups it opens on the same site (including common sign-in pop-ups).
  • Everything it records stays in your browser on your device. We have no servers and receive none of your data.
  • Data leaves your device only when you download a report file yourself. Every downloaded report ZIP includes the raw network data, redacted the same way as the report. Email addresses in it are masked, but it can still contain names, phone numbers and other personal data you typed or the site returned. The handover text that is then copied to your clipboard is not sent anywhere by the extension.
  • Screenshots are only partly masked. Before a screenshot is saved, Stepstack covers passwords, card numbers and other sensitive fields, but it can miss some, and it does not cover ordinary text you type, such as names or search terms. Record with a test account.

1. What Stepstack does

Stepstack is a Chrome extension that records a walkthrough of a website — for example a sign-up or onboarding flow — and turns it into a local report. The report shows the steps you took, a screenshot of each step, and the network and analytics requests the site made at each step.

2. When data is recorded

  • Recording starts only when you click the start-recording button (開始錄製) in the extension's pop-up or press its recording shortcut (Alt+Shift+R by default).
  • Only the tab where you started is recorded, plus tabs and pop-ups that a recorded tab opens, while their page is inside the recording's scope (below). A pop-up for one of the common sign-in or payment pages listed below is recorded too. A new tab that opens on another site is not recorded unless it moves into the scope.
  • A recording is limited to the site you started on (its domain and subdomains), any extra domain you add, and common sign-in and payment pages a flow passes through (for example Google, Apple and Microsoft sign-in, Stripe Checkout, PayPal). If the tab where you started goes to another site, the whole recording pauses. If another recorded tab goes to another site, nothing from that tab is recorded until it comes back.
  • While recording, Chrome shows a bar saying the extension is debugging the browser, and a REC indicator appears on the page. Cancelling Chrome's bar stops the recording.

Redaction and its limits

Before anything is saved, Stepstack tries to remove secrets: URL parameters and body fields whose names suggest passwords, tokens, sessions, cookies, keys, card data or one-time codes, and values that look like keys or tokens, are replaced with [redacted].

  • Email addresses are replaced with [email] in request and response bodies, in URLs, in page titles and headings, in the names of the buttons and fields you use, and in the page content summary. In the report ZIP, an email address you typed into a field is replaced with [email] too.
  • Card numbers, IBANs, Taiwan national ID numbers and phone numbers that Stepstack recognises are replaced with [card], [iban], [id] and [phone] in page text and in network data.
  • Values of masked fields. If a value you typed into a field stored as •••• shows up again in a request body, in a response body (values of 6 characters or more), or on a later page of the recording, it is replaced with [已輸入]. These values are kept only in the extension's memory while the recording runs and are never saved.
  • The llm folder of the report ZIP, which is written for AI agents, replaces the values you typed into fields with named placeholders such as {work_email}, in its list of actions and wherever it finds the same value again in page text. Its screenshots are the same masked screenshots as the rest of the report. The report and the raw folder keep the values you typed, except for the masks described above.

Screenshot masking

Before a screenshot is saved, Stepstack covers these with solid grey boxes: password, one-time code, card number and other fields whose values it stores as ••••; email fields; payment frames (for example Stripe or PayPal card fields); text on the page that it recognises as a card number, IBAN, Taiwan national ID number, phone number, email address, key or token, or two-factor recovery code; and elements the site marks as private. The unmasked image is never saved. If Stepstack cannot place the boxes, it saves the step without a screenshot.

Please be aware:

  • Redaction and masking are automatic and rule-based. They cannot be guaranteed to catch every secret or every piece of personal information. For example, an email address written in full-width characters is not recognised.
  • Redaction does not remove names, addresses, or other ordinary values you type from form values or network data. A phone number that Stepstack does not recognise, or that you typed into a form, also stays as it is. So the raw folder of the report ZIP can still contain this information: keep it on your computer and do not give it to a cloud AI tool.
  • Screenshot masking can miss things, for example content that is drawn inside embedded frames, or text it does not recognise. It does not cover ordinary text you type, such as names or search terms, and it does not cover messages or other content on the page. This applies to every screenshot, including those in the llm folder. Avoid showing sensitive information while recording, and use a test account.

Data present in the page but not recorded

So that it can mask what you type, the extension's page script runs on http and https pages and keeps the values you type on a page in that page's memory, even when you are not recording. These values are never saved or sent anywhere, and they disappear when you close or leave the page.

4. Where data is stored

  • Recordings, screenshots and generated reports are stored in the extension's own storage in your browser (IndexedDB) on your device.
  • The state of a recording in progress and an unsaved draft of your notes are kept in Chrome's session storage, which is cleared when the browser closes.
  • Your settings (theme, keyboard shortcuts, interface language) and which tips have been shown are kept in Chrome's local extension storage. The extension pages also keep a copy of these settings in their own local browser storage, so the right theme shows before a page finishes loading. If you open an exported report, its theme choice is kept in that browser's local storage.
  • The extension requests unlimited storage so that long recordings with many screenshots fit.

5. What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you export it. Stepstack has no servers. It does not send recordings, analytics, crash reports or usage data to us or to anyone else. It loads no code, fonts or other resources from the internet.

  • Downloaded reports. When you click the download button (下載 zip) on a report, a ZIP file with the report is saved to your computer. Every ZIP also includes a raw folder with the recording's raw network data: the requests the recorded pages made (to the site itself and to analytics services) and the responses they got back. This data goes through the same redaction as the report (see Redaction and its limits above), so it can still contain personal data the site returned, such as names, addresses or phone numbers. It leaves your device only if you share the ZIP. The ZIP also has an llm folder for AI agents, built from the same redacted recording, with the values you typed replaced by placeholders (see Redaction and its limits above). The ZIP also has a gtm folder with a storyboard and a draft how-to article for making a product video or guide. They are built from the same redacted recording and use only screen, button and field names; values you typed are left out, and screenshots are referenced by name, not copied again. What happens to the file afterwards — for example if you share it or give it to an AI tool — is up to you, and that recipient's own privacy terms apply.
  • Handover text on your clipboard. After the download finishes, the extension copies a short handover text to your clipboard, replacing what was there. It contains the location of the ZIP file on your computer, short instructions for an AI coding tool, and a reminder that the raw folder may contain personal data and should stay on your computer. The file location usually includes your user folder name (for example /Users/<name>/Downloads/…). The extension does not send this text anywhere; it stays on your device until you paste it somewhere. If copying does not work, a button to copy it appears instead. If your system syncs the clipboard to other devices, your system's settings apply.
  • Copy for FigJam. When you click Copy for FigJam (複製到 FigJam) on a report's user flow chart, the extension puts an image of the chart on your clipboard, and you can also copy the chart's Mermaid source text (複製 Mermaid 原始碼). Both show the screen titles and page paths from your recordings. Nothing is sent anywhere; they stay on your device until you paste them.
  • Links you click. If you click an external link inside a report, it opens in a new tab like any other link. The report-a-problem link (回報問題) on the extension's help page opens your own email app with a new message that has the extension version in its subject; nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself.
  • Other tools. If you use a separate dictation or speech-to-text tool to write your notes, that tool is not part of Stepstack. Stepstack itself does not access your microphone.

6. How data is used and shared

  • Recorded data is used only to show you your recordings and to build the reports you ask for.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
  • No person at Stepstack can read your data, because it never reaches us.
  • The use of information received through Chrome extension APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

  • Recordings are kept until you delete them. Open the extension's Recordings page (the 所有錄製 button in the pop-up), open the ⋯ menu next to the recording and choose delete this recording (刪除這次錄製); after a 5-second undo window, the recording, its screenshots and every report built from it are removed. In the pop-up, or on the Save recording page (儲存錄製) that opens after you stop recording, you can also discard the recording you just made (捨棄這次錄製); this removes what was already saved of it, together with any report built from it. If a recording could not be saved normally, discarding it keeps what was already stored; it then appears on the Recordings page as not finished (未正常結束), and you can delete it there.
  • Reports are built from your recordings and contain copies of their screenshots and network data. The extension keeps the 5 most recent reports; when a newer one is generated, the oldest is removed. Deleting a recording also deletes every report built from it. There is no separate button to delete a single report: delete a recording it was built from, or it is removed once 5 newer reports exist.
  • Session data (recording state, note drafts) is removed when you save or discard a recording, and when the browser closes.
  • Everything at once: uninstalling Stepstack (chrome://extensions → Remove) deletes all of its stored data from your browser.
  • Downloaded ZIP files are ordinary files on your computer. Delete them as you would any file.
